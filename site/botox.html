Botox

Softer lines, natural movement. A small amount of botulinum toxin relaxes the muscles that cause expression lines. Your doctor places it so you still look like you, only rested.

What it treats

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Crow's feet around the eyes
  • Lifting a heavy brow
  • Jaw tension and square jaw

What to expect

Consultation

Your doctor studies how your face moves and agrees the areas and dose with you.

Mapping

Injection points are marked so the result stays symmetrical.

Treatment

A few quick, fine injections. Most people feel a light pinch.

Aftercare

Stay upright for four hours, skip the gym for the day, and book a review at two weeks.

Questions

Will I look frozen?

Not when it is done well. Doses are set to soften lines while keeping expression, and you decide how much movement you want to keep.

Does it hurt?

It feels like a light pinch. A numbing cream is available if you prefer it.

When do I see the result?

Most people notice a change within a week, with the full effect at about two weeks.

Who should not have Botox?

People who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have certain muscle conditions. Your doctor checks your history at the consultation.
