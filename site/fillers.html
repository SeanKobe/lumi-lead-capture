Fillers

Volume and shape, placed with care. Hyaluronic acid fillers add volume where it has been lost and refine shape where you want it. We aim for balance across the face, so no single feature stands out.

What it treats

  • Lip definition and hydration
  • Cheek volume
  • Jawline and chin shape
  • Under-eye hollows
  • Nasolabial folds

What to expect

Consultation

We review your goals, photos and facial proportions, and agree what to treat first.

Plan

Your doctor picks the product and amount, and starts with less so you can add later.

Treatment

Filler is placed slowly with numbing for comfort. Most sessions take under an hour.

Aftercare

Avoid heat, alcohol and hard exercise for 24 hours. A review at two weeks fine tunes the result.

Questions

Is it reversible?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved by your doctor if you are not happy with the result.

Will it look overfilled?

We start conservatively and build up. Subtle is the default here.

How long does swelling last?

Mild swelling and small bruises are common for a few days. Most people go back to work the next day.

How much will I need?

It depends on the area. Your doctor gives an exact quote after the consultation, before anything is done.
